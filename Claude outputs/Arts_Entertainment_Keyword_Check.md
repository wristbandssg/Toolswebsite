## E. Coverage and review notes

**Needs review (not built):**

- AP Music Theory Score Calculator: Exam-score intent: belongs with the 21 existing tools in Jobs & Education > AP Score Calculators, not in Arts & Entertainment
- Movie Calculator: Unclear intent: could mean movie length, movie budget, ticket cost or "what movie to watch"; the specific movie tools in the list cover the measurable versions
- Music Calculator: Too vague: no single calculation; covered by the specific BPM, chord, interval and royalty tools
- Bacon Number Calculator: Needs a full film-cast database (IMDb-style graph) to compute degrees of separation; not a formula calculator
- Baddies Value Calculator: Game item trading values (Roblox "Baddies") change daily and need a live value database, not a formula
- Hifi Calculator: Unclear intent (hi-fi system sizing, audio file bitrate, or a game term); no single calculation
- BTS Calculator: Unclear intent (the K-pop group, a game, or "behind the scenes"); no identifiable calculation
- Spotify Age Calculator: Needs a listener's Spotify history (Spotify's own "listening age"); cannot be calculated from user inputs

**Functionality the merged tools must keep:**
- String Tension Calculator: guitar and bass presets, plus D'Addario and Stringjoy unit weights.
- Chord Calculator: triads, seventh chords and a piano-key view. Chord Inversion Calculator: triad inversions.
- Interval Calculator: semitone count, compound intervals and the inversion of an interval.
- Royalty Calculator: all platforms at once, DistroKid payout, and a reverse mode (streams needed for a target income).
- Playlist Calculator: album length and YouTube playlist length (with playback speed).
- Chord Progression Calculator: Roman numeral analysis and diatonic harmony for a key.
- Note Frequency Calculator: note ↔ Hz both ways, including drum tuning (Tune-Bot style) pitches.
- Tempo to MS Calculator: note and rhythm lengths in milliseconds.
- Compatibility Name Calculator: love, crush and prank-love modes (entertainment only).
- Destiny Matrix Arcana Calculator: personal arcana numbers from a birth date.

**Hard to build:** Zi Wei Dou Shu, Bazi, Qimen Dunjia, Human Design, Fame Astrology and Venus Line need Chinese-calendar solar terms or planetary positions (an ephemeris). They can be built, but with more effort than the other tools, and should be labelled for entertainment.

**Dated figures to refresh:** per-stream payout rates, the US mechanical royalty rate, ASCAP fee schedules and Vivid Seats fees change every year.
