**Duplicate check (how it was done)**
- **Exact:** the slug, the tool name, or a keyword already mapped to a tool is the same as an existing tool. For example, "Soap Lye" and "Freight Class" were removed.
- **Near:** the idea has the same words as an existing tool, give or take one word. For example, "Bearing Life L10" matched bearing-life and "Room Mode Frequency" matched room-mode.
- **Synonyms:** salary, income, take-home, PAYE and tax were treated as the same word, and so were fee, price and cost. For example, "Hong Kong Salaries Tax" matched hong-kong-income-tax and "Etsy Fees" matched etsy-fee.
- **Within the list:** near-identical pairs inside the list were removed too.
- The check is deliberately strict. When in doubt, an idea was removed.

**নতুন subcategories (proposed)**
- Health has only 2 subcategories today, so 4 new ones are proposed: Heart & Blood, Kidney, Liver & Lab Values, Medication & Dosage, and Body & Wellness.
- Finance > Tax gets 4 new regional subcategories: Middle East (GCC), Europe, Asia, and Latin America & Africa. There is no subcategory for these countries today.
- No new main category is proposed. Health is the main category you already approved.

**যাচাইয়ের সীমাবদ্ধতা**
- Before building, each batch will be re-checked against the database of that day, as per the usual rules.
- Health > Kidney, Liver & Lab Values and Medication & Dosage are clinical. Earlier you chose to skip prescriber-only tools, so please confirm these two groups before they are built.
- Restaurant nutrition tools need published menu data. If a chain has no public nutrition data, that tool will be skipped at build time.
