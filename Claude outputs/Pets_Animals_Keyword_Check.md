**Notes**

- Breed weight keywords (Yorkie, Chihuahua, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever, Great Dane…) share one puppy-weight method with a breed selector; doodle crosses (Goldendoodle, Bernedoodle, Cavapoo, Maltipoo, Aussiedoodle) share one parent-based doodle tool. - Medication tools are dosing references for owners and vets: each must show the label mg/kg range, frequency, warnings and "confirm with your veterinarian".
