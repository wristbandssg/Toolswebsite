## E. Coverage and review notes

**Needs review (not built):**

- Drink Size Calculator: Ambiguous: cup-size conversion, chain cup sizes or serving size; no single calculator intent
- Fermentation Calculator: Ambiguous: beer/wine fermentation time, sourdough or vegetable ferment; the specific tools (attenuation, brine, sourdough) cover each
- Miracle Salad Time Calculator: Unclear intent; no known calculator or formula behind this term

**Functionality the merged tools must keep:**
- Nutrition Calculator: per-food calories, protein, carbs, fiber and sugar by grams or servings.
- Recipe Nutrition Calculator: per-serving calories, macros, net carbs and a nutrition-facts style label.
- Coffee Calculator: brew-method presets (drip, pour over, French press) for the coffee-to-water ratio.
- Turkey Cook Time Calculator: whole bird, breast and spatchcock. Brine Calculator: turkey brine preset.
- Curing Salt Calculator: pastrami / corned-beef brine preset. Equilibrium Cure Calculator: dry-cure bacon preset.
- Alcohol Dilution Calculator: proofing down to a target proof. Alcohol Units Calculator: beer-to-shot equivalence.
- Pre Boil Gravity Calculator: dilute or concentrate wort to a target gravity.
- Party Food Calculator: side dishes for 20–100+ guests (pasta salad, fruit, spaghetti, chili, mashed potatoes).
- Jambalaya Calculator: gumbo option. Meal Macro Calculator: meal-prep batch mode. Serving Size Calculator: portion weight per person. Mexican Food Nutrition Calculator: taco builder.

**Brand nutrition and price tools:**
- Each of the 171 restaurant nutrition calculators needs that chain's current published menu nutrition data. Build only the ones with a usable official source, and label them unofficial ("not affiliated with ..."). Smaller chains (for example Dions, Cabo Bob's, Guac Time, Currito, Crazy Bowls & Wraps, Salsa Fresca, Barberitos) may not publish full data; those move to review if no source is found.
- Brand price calculators (McDonald's, Chipotle, In-N-Out, Starbucks) and Fast Food Price use typical US prices that vary by location, so they need an editable price field.
- Disney Dining Plan and Carnival drink package prices change every year; date them.

**Outside this category:** `cat-food-amount-calculator` and `dog-food-amount-calculator` sit in Shopping > Lifestyle, Party & Wedding; they belong in Pets & Animals.
